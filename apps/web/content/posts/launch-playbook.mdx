A strong starter removes the blank-page problem. Instead of beginning from scratch, you inherit a polished structure with sections already tuned for **clarity, trust, and conversion**.

## What to swap first

- Update the metadata in `site-data.ts`.
- Replace the demo copy on the home, services, and contact pages.
- Add the client's brand colors and imagery.
- Publish two to three useful blog posts for search visibility.

## Why this matters

When your foundation already covers SEO, performance, and mobile responsiveness, your energy shifts toward messaging and polish instead of wiring and cleanup.
