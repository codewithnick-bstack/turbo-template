Great SEO starts with a clear site structure. Home, about, services, blog, and contact pages each signal a different kind of intent.

## Start with the essentials

- Write specific page titles and descriptions.
- Add schema.org metadata for your business.
- Publish case studies and blog posts that answer buyer questions.
- Make your site fast on mobile networks.

These basics compound over time and give your client a much stronger foundation than a generic brochure site.
