### Second batch (18 photos, added 2026-09-04)

- All 18 verified on their own photo pages as plain **Unsplash License** or **Pexels
  License** (free commercial use). No Unsplash+/Getty-partner content was used.
- Note: this batch was sourced twice in parallel by two agent runs; the second run's
  downloads overwrote the first run's files on disk before the first run's manifest edit
  landed. This entry documents the files actually present on disk (the second run's picks).
- `bridge-girder.jpg` was shot in Hamburg, Germany (HafenCity) — a crawler crane mid-lift
  of a bridge segment; "LEONHARD WEISS" appears as small incidental text on a guardrail,
  not a prominent logo. No US-shot heavy-lift-in-progress frame existed under these
  licenses after an extensive search.
- `tunnel-works.jpg` and `surveyor.jpg` have no location stated on their photo pages;
  no US-identifying or foreign-language signage is visible in frame.
- `concrete-pour.jpg` is by the same photographer as the existing `trades.jpg` (Ümit
  Tektaş); no country stated on the photo page, generic heavy-civil composition.
- `earthworks.jpg` has no stated location; verified free of readable brand logos on the
  equipment (an earlier Volvo-branded and a John Deere-branded candidate were both
  rejected for prominent logos before landing on this one).
- `safety-briefing.jpg` — this subject was the hardest to source cleanly: multiple
  candidates were rejected for being crowd/rally photo-ops (Tata India site visit),
  large readable company logos ("General Demolition"), non-US signage (Jakarta), or
  duplicate framing of the same photoshoot series already used for `superintendent.jpg`
  (Mikael Blomkvist's 8961xxx set). Settled on a two-person foreman/worker review shot —
  close to but not exactly the "small group" brief — as the cleanest license-clear match.
- `facade-install.jpg` was shot in Munich, Germany. Two earlier candidates (a Pexels
  Hamburg shot, a Lisbon Unsplash shot) had readable brand text/logos and were rejected;
  this shows curtain-wall panels mid-install with a lift, protective wrap, no branding.
- `mechanical-room.jpg` and `industrial-shell.jpg` have no stated location; verified as
  genuine industrial/building-services interiors, not residential HVAC closeups.
- `paving.jpg` was shot in Idaho, USA (asphalt paver resurfacing a rural road) — confirmed
  US on the photo page.
- `energy-infra.jpg` was shot in Hamwarde, Schleswig-Holstein, Germany; generic wind
  turbine against blue sky, no identifying signage.
- `finished-building.jpg` and `residential-development.jpg` have no stated location;
  generic modern commercial exterior / aerial multi-building development respectively.
- `field-office.jpg` is an architect's desk flat-lay (hard hat, blueprints, drafting
  tools) rather than a site-trailer desk with a laptop — the closest license-clear match
  after extensive search; no laptop-plus-blueprints-plus-no-people combination on an
  actual jobsite (not a design studio) was found under these licenses. Kept in its native
  portrait crop since cropping to landscape cut off too much of the composition.
